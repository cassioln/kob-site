    Preparation

    How do I check my terminal arrival window and port of call schedule?

    Kriativos On Board takes place November 20–23, 2026, on MSC Musica, departing and returning to Santos with a call in Búzios. Check the day and time of the call, arrival window, and boarding instructions on your booking itinerary and voucher. Do not use the bus charter schedule as the ship's boarding time.

    Source: Booking itinerary
    Who requests cabin vouchers, when, and through which channel?

    Royal Trip manages group check-in issuance. When announced in the group chat, the cabin lead should contact Júnior privately to receive vouchers and luggage tags in PDF format. Verify passenger details and print documents. There is no guaranteed release date; follow travel agency announcements.

    Which identification document should I bring? Is digital ID accepted?

    For Brazilian citizens on this domestic route, MSC accepts RG, CNH, passport, and other recognized civil documents in good condition with a recognizable photo. Digital driver's licenses must be opened within the official government app (screenshots/copies are not accepted). Bringing physical documents is strongly recommended. Foreign travelers should bring valid passports.

    Source: MSC Documents [S02]
    What documentation is required for minors and foreign passengers?

    Confirm specific requirements with Royal Trip based on age, accompanying guardians, nationality, and residency status. Obtain official notarized authorization forms where required in advance. Do not submit personal ID copies through public forms.

    Source: MSC Documents [S02]
    I am pregnant: what medical clearance is required?

    MSC does not permit travel if pregnancy reaches 24 weeks at any point during the cruise. Below this limit, MSC requires an official medical certificate confirming fitness to travel and low-risk pregnancy, stating gestational age and estimated delivery date, signed and stamped by an authorized physician. Notify Royal Trip in advance.

    Source: MSC Health & Accessibility Policy [S03]
    How do I verify baby registration and request a crib?

    All guests, including infants, must be included in the cabin booking. Minicruises permit infants under MSC regulations. Confirm fares, port taxes, crib availability, and policies with Royal Trip; do not assume automatic free admission.

    Source: MSC Children Policy [S08]
    Do I need COVID-19 vaccine or test certificates?

    Check current sanitary protocols with Royal Trip and MSC closer to departure. Health regulations depend on maritime authorities and destination requirements. Always follow official guidelines issued for your specific sailing.

    Source: MSC Travel Requirements [S02/S03]
    What is the official MSC luggage allowance?

    Per passenger: up to two checked bags of max 23 kg (50 lbs) each (max dimensions 90 × 75 × 43 cm) and up to two carry-on pieces of max 10 kg (22 lbs) each (max dimensions 56 × 45 × 25 cm). Total luggage per cabin must not exceed 100 kg or 8 bags. Also verify separate flight or bus limits if applicable.

    Source: Official MSC Baggage Policy [S01]
    How do I drop off luggage and what stays with me?

    Affix agency-issued MSC tags to all checked bags before arriving at the terminal. Keep identification, travel vouchers, medications, valuables, and essential clothing in your carry-on luggage, as checked bags are delivered to cabins later in the day.

    Source: MSC Baggage Guidelines [S01]
    What items are prohibited? Are hair dryers, straighteners, or drones allowed?

    MSC prohibits weapons, illegal substances, inflammable items, and heat appliances like clothing irons. Hair dryers and hair straighteners are permitted. Drones may be brought on board if stored securely in the cabin, but flight from the ship is strictly prohibited. Commercially packaged dry snacks are generally allowed.

    Source: MSC Baggage Policy [S01]
    What amenities does the cabin provide and what should I bring?

    Cabins include bed linens, towels, body wash, shampoo, hair dryer, TV, air conditioning, and safe. Bring personal toiletries such as toothbrush, toothpaste, and hair conditioner, plus sunscreen and compatible power plug adapters. Cabin minibars do not guarantee temperatures suitable for storing medicines.

    Source: MSC Musica specifications [S07]

    Arrival

    How does the official KOB bus charter work?

    The charter operates round-trip between Barra Funda (São Paulo) and the Port of Santos. Meeting point: Rua Tagipuru, near nº 552. Bus 1: meet 06:00, depart 06:30, tolerance 06:40. Bus 2: meet 06:40, depart 07:20, tolerance 07:30. Arrive on time; tolerance is not a second departure time.

    Bus 1 is confirmed. Bus 2 depends on minimum passenger threshold with full refund via Pix if not reached. Return from Santos on Nov 23 is scheduled for 10:30.

    Arriving by plane: Is there KOB airport transfer from GRU or CGH?

    The KOB charter departs strictly from Barra Funda, not from airports. Travelers flying into Guarulhos (GRU) or Congonhas (CGH) can consult regular commercial bus services (such as Viação Cometa) to Santos bus station, then take local transport to Concais. Allow generous time for holiday traffic.

    Source: Viação Cometa [S10]
    Driving by car: How does port parking work?

    Official terminal parking is operated directly by Concais in Santos with advance online reservation recommended (concais.com.br). Check rates and access guidelines directly with the terminal. Follow signage for passenger drop-off upon arrival.

    Source: Concais Terminal [concais.com.br]
    Can I access my cabin immediately after boarding?

    Cabins may still be undergoing turnover and cleaning. Readiness may be around 13:00/14:00; wait for the announcement over the ship’s public address system. Enjoy the lunch buffet while waiting and keep essentials with you.

    Is the onboard safety drill mandatory?

    Yes. International maritime safety regulations mandate that all embarking passengers complete the safety muster drill as instructed by MSC crew and in-cabin guides.

    Source: MSC Safety Operations

    Games & activities

    Where and when do I pick up my welcome kit? What does it include?

    Welcome kit pickup is scheduled for 16:00/16:30 on day one at Crystal Lounge (location subject to final confirmation). The kit includes an event lanyard, wristband, collectible metal coin, and a board game tote bag from Fácil Shopping.

    Where can I find the gaming schedule and official notices?

    Look for the bulletin board at the entrance of the gaming lounge and follow the official group messaging channel. MSC's daily program also lists general ship activities.

    How do I verify lounge hours and Sunday return deadlines?

    Crystal Lounge offers open seating during daytime and evening gaming blocks. There is no promise of 24h uninterrupted gaming. On Sunday, we will announce the game return cutoff for final inventory.

    How do I register on Board Game Guru and connect with monitors?

    Board Game Guru is the official event companion app (iOS/Android). Open the KOB 2026 page and click ‘I’m going’ to enter prize raffles and view the available games library.

    I cannot use the app. Can I still participate in raffles?

    Yes. Approach the Encounter monitor desk in the gaming lounge to request manual registration on the participant list. Lack of app access will not exclude you from raffle participation.

    Can I bring personal games and prototypes, bookmark titles, and share my collection?

    Yes! Register games you bring in the Guru app (importable from Ludopedia or BGG). Favorite games you wish to play to help monitors plan tables. Mark personal games clearly and respect baggage limits.

    How do I borrow games and can I play them outside the lounge?

    Follow monitor instructions for checkout. If taking a game outside Crystal Lounge, register your name and cabin number with the desk. Please keep games in permitted relaxation areas and do not occupy dining restaurant tables for gaming.

    I booked solo or want to join a table: how do I find players?

    Ask Encounter monitors in the gaming lounge to match you with players and introduce you to games. You do not need to arrive with a pre-formed group.

    During the cruise

    What should I wear? Is formal gala attire required?

    Bring comfortable casual clothes for daytime gaming and a light sweater for breezy decks and AC. Dinner in the main dining room is smart casual (sleeveless tank tops and flip-flops are restricted). Tuxedos or formal gala gowns are not required.

    How do I prepare for theme parties?

    The White Party (Festa do Branco) is a cruise tradition; bring white clothing if you wish to join. For KOB's Sunday night farewell, the announced theme is 80s Retro (neon and vintage accessories). Costume participation is completely optional.

    Where do I check meal times and alternatives to my assigned dinner shift?

    Full-board meals are included in the buffet and main dining rooms. The buffet provides extended hours including pizza and snacks; exact operating hours appear in the MSC daily ship program. Specialty dining incurs separate charges.

    Source: MSC Musica Dining Guidelines
    How does my dinner shift work? What if I arrive late?

    Your Cruise Card indicates your assigned main dining room shift and table number. Arrive promptly; kitchen orders allow a 15-minute window before courses advance. If you miss your seating, the buffet provides casual dining alternatives.

    Can I dine together with friends in other cabins?

    Immediately after boarding, speak with the restaurant maître d' providing the cabin numbers of your travel group to request table consolidation. The dining team will confirm whether there is room.

    How do beverage packages work and what are the daily limits?

    Drink packages are optional. Options include Non-Alcoholic, Easy, and Premium Extra. If booked, all adults sharing a cabin must contract the same package; minors must have the corresponding child package. Alcoholic packages include up to 15 alcoholic beverages per 24-hour cycle (6:00 to 6:00 next day).

    Source: MSC Beverage Policies [S04]
    Is the cabin minibar included in beverage packages?

    No. Items consumed directly from the in-cabin minibar are billed separately to your account and are not included in standard packages.

    Source: MSC Beverage Terms [S04/S06]
    How do I purchase Wi-Fi internet? Does mobile signal work in Búzios?

    Satellite internet packages are purchased separately per device and are locked to the specific hardware registered upon activation (cannot be transferred or shared). In Búzios, Brazilian mobile cellular signal may be available depending on carrier and location.

    Source: MSC Onboard Services [S06]
    Can I use the MSC for Me app and chat without an internet package?

    Yes. Connect to the ship's internal Wi-Fi network without purchasing internet to use MSC for Me features and internal chat (for guests 16+). Adding "Kriativo" to your display name helps friends find you.

    Source: MSC for Me [S05]
    How do I settle onboard expenses? Are international cards accepted?

    Purchases on board are charged to your Cruise Card. Link a physical credit/debit card (including enabled international cards) or settle with a cash deposit at reception. Check currency and activation requirements with your bank prior to boarding.

    Source: MSC Onboard Account
    How do I book specialty restaurants on this sailing?

    Specialty venues such as Kaito Sushi Bar operate separately from full board. MSC lists Butcher’s Cut debuting on MSC Musica in late 2026. Verify operational restaurants and reserve a table directly on board.

    Source: MSC Musica specifications [S07]
    What should I check regarding travel insurance coverage?

    Travel insurance is recommended to cover unforeseen cancellations or onboard medical assistance. Request the official policy from Royal Trip and check coverage limits, currencies, deductibles, and deadlines before purchasing.

    Source: Royal Trip Insurance Policy
    My medication requires refrigeration or special needs. What should I do?

    Inform Royal Trip in advance via private message so requests can be forwarded to MSC medical staff. Do not rely on the cabin minibar for precise temperature storage. Keep medicines in your carry-on luggage.

    Source: MSC Special Needs [S03]
    How does the kids club work? Does staff change diapers or provide meals?

    MSC offers kids clubs organized by age groups. Children wearing diapers must be accompanied by a guardian during general recreation activities, as youth counselors do not provide diaper changing or meals. Confirm specific services with youth staff on board.

    Source: MSC Children Guidelines [S08]
    Are water, tea, and coffee complimentary? Will we have the Aqua system?

    Water dispensers, hot water, tea, and drip coffee are available in the buffet restaurant during service hours. The Aqua reusable bottle system availability on MSC Musica should be confirmed with the ship crew.

    Disembarkation

    What time must I vacate my cabin on Monday morning?

    Cabins must be vacated early to prepare for the subsequent sailing. Follow the exact time specified in the onboard disembarkation program delivered to your cabin on the final evening.

    Source: MSC Disembarkation Policy
    How do I pack and collect luggage for disembarkation?

    Follow MSC color-coded luggage tags and instructions provided in your cabin. Leave checked luggage outside your door by the indicated hour on Sunday night. Retrieve luggage in the terminal customs hall in Santos.

    Source: MSC Baggage Guidelines [S01]
    Should companions join official groups? Whom should I contact?

    Add travel companions and cabin family members to the official broadcast group to receive event announcements. Contact the cabin lead or our team to request invitation links. For booking/vouchers, message Royal Trip privately.
