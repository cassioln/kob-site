Boarding, Check-In & Documents

What is the date and time of boarding?
Boarding begins on November 20 (Friday) from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM at the Port of Santos. Disembarkation is on November 23 (Monday) starting at 7:00 AM.
What documents do I need for boarding?
10 days prior to boarding, online check-in will open on the MSC app. Upon completion, you will receive a QR Code voucher to present at the terminal along with your valid passport or government ID.
When can I complete check-in?
Online check-in opens 10 days before sailing. We will provide full step-by-step guidance. The primary booking contact can check in for everyone in their cabin.
Are vaccines required for boarding?
Please consult standard cruise health guidelines closer to departure. Antigen tests with lab certification are accepted if required by maritime health authorities.
How and when do I check my luggage?
Luggage drop-off is the first step upon arriving at the terminal. Port staff will tag and deliver your suitcases directly to your cabin.
Is there an official shuttle bus to the port?
Yes! We offer a dedicated round-trip charter bus from São Paulo directly to the Port of Santos. Learn more about the charter bus and book your seat here.
How do I arrange parking at the port?
Advance reservations are highly recommended. Details for the official Concais port parking and nearby hotels are listed in our Travel & Accommodations section.
Who is the travel agency Royal Trip?
Royal Trip is our official licensed travel agency partner, with over a decade of experience in maritime tourism, cruise logistics, and corporate events (CNPJ: 16.957.402/0001-19).
What is the itinerary for MSC Musica?
Departs Santos on Nov 20, sails to Búzios-RJ, and returns to Santos on Nov 23, 2026.

Onboard Experience & Gaming

Will there be a dedicated game room?
Yes! A private hall reserved exclusively for our KOB group. Tables are open-seating for all attendees to join and play.
Which games will be available in the library?
Our curated game library features 350+ modern titles, party games, heavy strategy, and prototypes. Attendees are also encouraged to bring their favorite personal games.
Can I bring my own board games and prototypes?
Absolutely! You can bring as many board games, expansions, and prototypes as you like.
Can I take games to other areas of the ship?
Yes, to pool decks, lounges, and sun areas (except dining tables during meal services). Please remember to check out and return games responsibly.
What are the operating hours of the game room?
The game room is planned to remain open around the clock (24/7), with specific quiet hours and tournament schedules confirmed closer to departure.
Is the safety drill mandatory?
Yes, international maritime law requires every passenger to complete the mandatory safety drill, easily done via the MSC mobile app followed by muster check.
How does a board game cruise differ from traditional conventions?
Kriativos On Board combines your favorite hobby with a world-class luxury vacation: all meals included, ocean views, resort entertainment, and non-stop gaming with a warm, welcoming community.

Food, Drinks & Cabins

What is included in my reservation?
Cabin accommodation in your selected category, full-board gourmet meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), and buffet beverages (water, tea, coffee).
Is food unlimited on the ship?
Yes! Multiple dining venues and buffet stations serve generous meals throughout the day and late into the night at no extra cost.
How does seated dinner service work?
Every evening, enjoy a multi-course seated dining experience (appetizer, main course, dessert) at our group's assigned dining seating.
Am I required to buy a drink package?
No, drink packages are optional. However, if one guest in a cabin purchases an alcoholic package, all adult cabin mates must share the same package per cruise line policy.
What adult beverage packages are available?
Non-Alcoholic, Easy, and Premium Extra packages, offered with special advance purchase discounts through our agency.
Does the cabin include a minibar?
Yes, MSC Musica cabins are equipped with a minibar. Consumption is billed separately unless included in specific suite tiers.
Is there a strict formal dress code for dinner?
Smart casual is perfect! For special evenings like Gala Night or the White Party, dressing to theme is encouraged but never mandatory.
How do onboard payments and currency work?
All onboard expenses are cashless, linked to your cruise cabin key card via international credit card or cash deposit (USD) at ship kiosks.

Insurance, Health & Rules

Is travel insurance required?
While optional, comprehensive travel medical insurance is strongly recommended for international maritime travel. Contact Royal Trip for easy add-on coverage.
What if I take medications that require refrigeration?
Please notify the travel agency in advance so medical refrigeration arrangements can be prepared for your cabin.
What items are prohibited on board?
Drones, alcoholic/non-alcoholic beverages brought from home, clothing irons, heating appliances, and weapons are prohibited. Cabins include built-in hairdryers.
What themed parties take place onboard?
Highlights include the iconic White Party (pack white clothing!) and a special Kriativa Costume & Cosplay Party. Full entertainment schedule will be shared before sailing.
