      Bus 1 is confirmed. Bus 2 and any additional vehicles require a minimum number of passengers. If it is not reached, the organizers will issue a full refund.

      Fleet status & minimum quorum

      • Bus 1: Target reached and 100% confirmed.
      • Bus 2: Over 50% of available seats already filled.

      If the minimum threshold for Bus 2 is not met, 100% of the amount paid via Pix will be fully refunded by the organization.

      Cancellation & seat transfer

      • Full refund: within 7 days of Pix payment (CDC).
      • Free transfer: reassign your seat to a friend up to 5 days before the trip.

      • Bus 2: Meet at 06:40 AM · Departure 07:20 AM (Seats Available!)
